# Spec Delta

## Purpose
The catalogue of the 24 portal games: which day each opens, its title, its gameplay kit, its signature mechanics and its win condition.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Twenty-four portals
The game SHALL contain exactly 24 portals numbered 1–24, each played in first-person 3D, each tied to the calendar day of the same number.

#### Scenario: Catalogue completeness
- **WHEN** the portal registry is listed
- **THEN** it contains 24 entries with days 1 through 24 and no duplicates

### Requirement: Portal 01 Sleighed!
Portal 1 SHALL be "Sleighed!", a first-person FPS shooter with dense interactive props, remote-detonated explosives, shrink-and-stomp weapon and jetpack, built on the FPS kit, and SHALL be won when the player destroys the Light Siphon on the mall roof.

#### Scenario: Portal 01 win condition
- **WHEN** the player destroys the Light Siphon on the mall roof
- **THEN** portal 1 reports a won result and awards at least 1 snowflake

### Requirement: Portal 02 Aurora Carpet
Portal 2 SHALL be "Aurora Carpet", a first-person carpet flight over deformable terrain, mana collection by carrier balloons and a growing castle, built on the Vehicle kit, and SHALL be won when the player owns at least 75 % of the world's mana or destroys the rival wizard's castle.

#### Scenario: Portal 02 win condition
