# Proposal

## Why

Day 1 opens on 1 December 2026 and must be a complete, original portal game:
*Sleighed!*, an FPS homage set in the Glitterdome Mall (concept:
`docs/concept/portals/week-1.md`, Day 1).

## What Changes

- Add the Day 1 portal on top of the FPS kit and a new text level format.
- Extend the FPS kit: per-portal weapon/enemy tables, ammo types, remote
  bombs, shrink-and-stomp, jetpack, breakables/objectives, usable props.
- Mark portal 1 as shipped so it opens on its date without the override.

Out of scope (garnish): mirrors, earthquake script, destructible lamps,
co-op/deathmatch modes, XR.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `portal-01-sleighed`: rules, goal, scoring and content of Day 1.
- `fps-levels`: the text level format that FPS portals load from `assets/maps/`.

### Modified Capabilities
- none

## Impact

`src/kits/fps/` (combat, map), `src/portals/p01_*`, `assets/maps/p01_sleighed.map`
(generated by `tools/mapgen_p01.py`), registry and tests.
